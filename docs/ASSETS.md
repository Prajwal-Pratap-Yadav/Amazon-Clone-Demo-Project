# Asset provenance

Current product illustrations and the hero still life are original geometric SVG drawings generated by `node scripts/generate_assets.mjs`, covered by the existing MIT license. The favicon is an original geometric mark. No third-party images, fonts, company marks or image metadata are embedded. Typography uses system fonts.

Four original files (`Amazon-Logo-312.png`, `Top picks for India.jpg`, `box_4.jpg`, `first_image.jpg`) and `Documantation File.pdf` lacked redistribution provenance. They are retired from the current tree, not relicensed. Their hashes are recorded in `legacy/original/manifest.json`; history is unchanged. The baseline retains the original broken-path behavior rather than repairing it before measurement.

Original HTML/CSS keep their exact bytes for characterization and are not included in the production bundle. Screenshots in `reports` come from actual scripted browser runs, with source/runner manifests, not mockups. See [methodology](measurements.md).
